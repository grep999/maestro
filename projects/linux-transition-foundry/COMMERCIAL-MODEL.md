# Commercial model and unit economics

## Product ladder

1. Readiness assessment: fixed scope, paid discovery.
2. Pilot: fixed cohort and acceptance criteria.
3. Transformation: priced by endpoints, complexity, applications and locations.
4. Managed operations: recurring per-endpoint or service-tier fee.
5. Adaptation engineering: separately scoped work for specialist applications and integrations.

## Pricing discipline

Do not publish fixed migration prices until delivery effort is measured. Quote from a transparent work breakdown:
- Discovery and architecture.
- Application remediation.
- Identity and infrastructure integration.
- Baseline engineering and automation.
- Pilot and training.
- Rollout labour and hypercare.
- Ongoing support and third-party costs.

Separate pass-through licences and hardware from engineering fees. Define scope, assumptions, exclusions, service levels, data processing, IP ownership, warranty, change control and exit/portability.

## TCO model

Compare the current and target environments over the same 3–5 year period and service levels.

Include:
- Operating system and application subscriptions.
- Hardware refresh and useful-life extension, only where supported by hardware condition.
- IT administration and support hours.
- Migration, training and coexistence.
- Specialist Windows licences, VDI/RDS and exception devices.
- Security, backup, monitoring and management tooling.
- Downtime, productivity and risk assumptions.
- Exit, data portability and future migration costs.

Show base, optimistic and pessimistic scenarios. Clearly distinguish cash savings, avoided costs and non-financial benefits. Validate assumptions with customer invoices, time records and pilot measurements.

## Initial customer profile

Start with organisations of approximately 10–50 endpoints whose workflows are predominantly browser-based, email, office documents, file shares and standard peripherals. Avoid highly specialised regulated or Windows-dependent environments as the first reference customer unless the required expertise and validation are available.

## First commercial objective

Secure one paid readiness assessment, then one paid pilot. Use actual delivery data to refine scope and pricing before scaling.
